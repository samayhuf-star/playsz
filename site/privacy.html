This policy describes how Playsz (“the app”) handles information. Playsz is an offline video player. It is published by Samay Vashsht.

What the app collects

Playsz does not collect personal information on our servers. The app has no backend and no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting service.

Playback position, settings, and a local record of whether you have unlocked Pro stay on your device. Those values are not uploaded to us.

To play videos you already have, the app reads your device’s video library. It does not upload your videos, photos, or files.

Purchases

Optional Playsz Pro is sold through Google Play Billing or the Apple App Store. Google or Apple processes the payment. The app stores an on-device entitlement after a successful purchase or restore. We do not receive your payment card details.

Permissions

The app asks for access to videos so it can play them. Picture-in-picture and notifications are requested only if you turn on a feature that needs them. The app does not ask for location, camera, or contacts.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. We do not share your information with advertisers or analytics companies. Store purchases are handled by Google or Apple under their own policies.

Retention and deletion

Because the app’s data lives on your device, you can clear it from the device (and, once shipped, from in-app Settings). Restoring a purchase from the store will unlock Pro again if the store still shows a valid purchase. Uninstalling the app removes the local data.

Children

Playsz is not directed at children and is not a children’s app.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
